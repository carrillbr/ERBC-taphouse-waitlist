Privacy Policy

Elk River Brewing Co. TapHouse is operated by ERBC LLC. Last updated October 24, 2026

Text messages

When you join the waitlist at Elk River Brewing Co. TapHouse and agree to texts, we send messages about your table only:

  • a confirmation with a link to follow your place in line,
  • a text when your table is ready, and
  • a reply if you text us to cancel.

That is usually two or three messages per visit. We don't send marketing texts. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies.

Reply STOP at any time to stop all texts. Reply HELP for help, or call us at (410) 656-7097. Reply C to give up your spot in line. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

What we collect

Your name, party size, mobile number, any notes you give the host, and your email address if you choose to share it. We also keep a record of the texts we send you and your visit times.

How we use it

We use this information to run the waitlist, to recognize returning guests, and to understand how busy we are. If you check the Brew Crew box, we will also email you about new beers, food and events. You can unsubscribe from those emails at any time.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

We do not sell, rent or share your mobile number or text opt-in with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile information and text consent are never shared. We use a texting provider (Twilio) only to deliver our messages to you.

Questions or deletion

To ask what we have on file or to have your information deleted, call (410) 656-7097 or ask the host at 18 S Main St, North East, MD 21901.
